Ratings Fixer

Writes stricter Custom Ratings so parental controls work on old and unrated titles. The official rating is never touched, and metadata refreshes don't overwrite Custom Ratings. Always run Preview first and read the report below.

Back up Jellyfin's database before your first Apply (Dashboard → Backups, or copy jellyfin.db with the server stopped). Apply edits thousands of items at once; the ledger can re-apply ratings, but only a backup can undo them.

Rules

Rules win over every source. For example: collection “Adult” → XXX, which hides those items from every account with a maximum rating set.

Behaviour

Consensus stops one unusually strict country from re-rating a title on its own. Rules always win regardless.
The US scale jumps from PG-13 (13) to R (17). On: a 15 becomes PG-13 and a 16 becomes R. Off: anything above 13 becomes R. A foreign 18 becomes R either way — NC-17 and XXX only come from rules or an actual NC-17.
Recommended. When off, a source that rates a title more leniently can lower it too.
